% ---------- Main body ----------
\section{Introduction}
\subsection{Purpose}
What this white paper is for, who it is for, and how to use it.

\subsection{Background and context}
A short narrative plus bullets:
\begin{itemize}
  \item What has changed (technology / regulation / market)
  \item Why now
  \item What success looks like
\end{itemize}

\section{Problem statement}
Define the problem crisply. Add evidence and citations \parencite{somekey}.

\section{Approach}
Describe your proposed approach, principles, and constraints.

\subsection{Operating model}
People/process/technology view.

\subsection{Delivery plan}
You can include a simple table:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l l X@{}}
\toprule
Phase & Duration & Output \\
\midrule
Discovery & 4--6 weeks & Prioritised use cases and validated requirements \\
Pilot & 8--12 weeks & Live, constrained systems with governance controls \\
Scale \& embed & Ongoing & Capability building and operational governance \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\section{Metrics and evaluation}
Include formulas if needed:
\[
E = 1 - \frac{T_1}{T_0}
\qquad
S = N (T_0 - T_1)
\]
Explain how you will measure impact and risk.

\begin{riskbox}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Risk:} Poor data quality \\
        \textbf{Mitigation:} Data profiling, minimum quality gates, feedback loops.
  \item \textbf{Risk:} Unclear ownership \\
        \textbf{Mitigation:} Named business owners, RACI, governance cadence.
\end{itemize}
\end{riskbox}

\section{Recommendations}
A short list of concrete recommendations, each with an owner and next step.
